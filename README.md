# Retail-IQ

## Project Description
Retail IQ is a complete retail analytics dashboard built in Power BI to track sales, inventory, customer feedback, and logistics across 33 store locations. Raw data from multiple business areas was cleaned in Power Query and organized into a galaxy schema to connect sales, inventory, returns, marketing, and logistics tables smoothly. The dashboard features four dedicated pages; Sales Overview, Product & Inventory, Customer & Returns, and Stores & Shipping, giving managers clear visibility into performance metrics to drive smarter business decisions.

## Business Challenges & Key Questions
I built this project to show how combining separate operational datasets helps answer core retail questions:

- How are total revenue and profit margins performing across different geographic regions and individual store outlets?
- Which products bring in the highest profits and margins, and how effectively is inventory turning over?
- What are the main drivers behind customer returns, and which regions suffer from the highest return rates?
- Which product categories receive the lowest customer review ratings, pointing to possible quality issues?
- Are regional warehouses meeting their on-time delivery commitments, and how are marketing budgets distributed across channels?
- How close are retail outlets to meeting their overall sales targets?

## Key Insights & Findings

- **Overall Sales & Region Performance:** Total revenue reached $7.15M across 6,000 orders with a 34% profit margin. North America led all regions by generating $4.30M in revenue ($1.46M profit), while Electronics was the top revenue category, accounting for 53.83% ($3.85M) of total sales.
- **Top Outlets:** Large urban flagship stores drove the bulk of earnings, with Chicago Flagship 3 making the highest profit at $149K ($437K revenue), followed by Los Angeles Flagship 3 ($143K profit) and Seattle Flagship ($141K profit).
- **Product Profitability & Margins:** Pulse Smartwatch and Nova Laptop 3 generated the highest absolute profit ($54K each). However, Shoes delivered the highest overall profit margin at 65%, followed by Office Chairs (41%) and Laptops (35%).
- **Return Trends & Drivers:** The business saw an average return rate of 9.02%. Returns were highest in Latin America, where Electronics hit a 33% return rate and Apparel reached 20%. The main reasons cited for returns were items damaged in transit, better prices found elsewhere, and wrong items shipped.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Across 1,000 customer reviews, the average rating was 4.03 out of 5. Laptops scored the highest customer satisfaction (4.50), while Desks (3.50) and Shoes (3.55) had the lowest satisfaction levels.
- **Logistics & Delivery:** On time delivery averaged 95% overall. The Middle East & Africa Distribution Center achieved a perfect 100% on time delivery rate, whereas the APAC Distribution Center fell behind at 91%.
- **Marketing Budget & Target Attainment:** Total marketing spend of $682K was spread evenly across four channels, led by Paid Search ($175K) and Email ($173K). Across all 33 outlets, sales target attainment reached 97%.
